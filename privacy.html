البيانات المحفوظة على جهازك

تُحفظ إعداداتك وسجل الصلوات وعدّادات الأذكار والتسبيح في التخزين المحلي لجهازك فقط، وتُحذف بإلغاء تثبيت التطبيق أو بمسح بيانات الموقع من المتصفح.

الإشعارات

خدمات خارجية

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Samt is a prayer times, Qibla direction and adhkar app. It is designed to run on your device, with no accounts and no servers of our own.

Data we collect

We do not collect any personal data, we do not send any information to servers of ours, and we use no tracking or advertising tools.

Location

If you choose “Use my current location”, the app asks your device for location permission to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. The coordinates are used on your device only and are never sent anywhere. You can instead pick your city from the list without granting this permission.

Data stored on your device

Your settings, prayer log and adhkar and tasbeeh counters are kept in your device’s local storage only. They are removed when you uninstall the app or clear the site’s data in your browser.

Notifications

If you turn on alerts, the app asks for notification permission to show prayer-time alerts on your device. Alerts are created on your device and do not pass through any server.

Third-party services

On first launch the app loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives standard technical information such as your IP address, under the Google Privacy Policy; the fonts are then stored on your device. The app is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical data when pages load, under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children

The app collects no data from any user, including children.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with its update date.
